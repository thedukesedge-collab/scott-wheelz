SNOOZE LOOK

HEADLIGHTS OFF = always off. RIDING LIGHTS while engaged, RUNNING LIGHTS otherwise; 0% = off. Same as Refloat's Headlight Brightness (riding) and Front Brightness (idle). SNOOZE BREATHING: lights slowly breathe while snoozing; off = dark.

RIDING: while engaged. IDLE: otherwise. 0% = off; HEAD/TAIL LIGHTS OFF = always off. Same as Refloat's Headlight Brightness (riding) and Front Brightness (idle). SNOOZE uses VESC's power-off idle clock: riding or rolling the wheel restarts it and wakes the lights. When to snooze is set on the LEDS page (SNOOZE). BREATHE: a slow breath; DIM: a steady low glow; DARK: off. An SLCM light module snoozes by itself; this is for controller-driven LEDs (e.g. Thor).

Same as Refloat's Status Idle Brightness (idle) and Status Brightness (riding).

⚠ FLASH LCM FIRMWARE

This rewrites the firmware of your board's light control module (LCM), the part that also switches the board's power.

• Only for light modules (Floatwheel LCM board, tested target: XRV) that already have the SLCM bootloader (SLCM.hex, flashed once with an ST-Link). Stock LCM firmware can't be flashed from the app.
• Board powered on, idle, on a stand. Do not ride, step on it or switch it off until it says Done (about a minute).
• Headlights and beeper are off while it flashes. Keep this app open and connected.
• If it's interrupted, the LCM keeps the board on and waits: press FLASH LCM again (power-cycle first if needed). Hold the button 2 s to power off.
• Experimental, at your own risk. Keep an ST-Link and your old LCM firmware as a fallback.
